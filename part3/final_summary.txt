Caveats
  - Seeds are independent pretraining runs; SFT used one fixed data order.
  - With 3 vs 3 seeds the smallest possible permutation p is 0.10; the four metrics are correlated.
  - GQA has a wider FFN and a slightly higher pretraining loss; larger drops go with higher clean accuracy.
  - M4 is computed for the closed tasks only; the Dolly verdict uses M1-M3.
  - Chronology: M1 had been seen before the decision rule was written. The three-metric rule (M1-M3) was
    declared before M2 and M3 were computed. M4 and the S_ metrics were added after the M1-M3 results were
    seen, so they are reported in full and cannot overturn the verdict.
  - Dolly metrics are secondary: ROUGE-L is crude, and its M3 and M4 are defined differently from the closed tasks.
